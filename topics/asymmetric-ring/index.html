Coarsening in the totally asymmetric regime

Overview · Digested · Raw

Motion and mass exchange

On a ring, a particle at site \(x\) jumps only to \(x+1\), at rate \(\eta_x(d_L+\eta_{x+1})\). An isolated cluster of \(m\) particles sends its first particle to the next empty site at rate \(d_Lm\). The attraction draws the rest of the cluster to that site. In the regime where these transfers are fast, a larger cluster moves faster and can catch a smaller one. The two clusters can exchange particles as the larger cluster passes. Cao, Chleboun and Grosskinsky (2014), Section 4

Research question

Can we derive an effective law for the masses and positions during coarsening? The first step is to quantify the mass exchange in a single encounter and determine when a sequence of encounters admits a limit.
