Condensation and Inclusion

Home · Topics · Literature

The inclusion process is a stochastic particle system in which particles attract one another. Particles jump at a rate that increases with the number of particles at the destination. This attraction can cause particles to cluster, a phenomenon known as condensation. The inclusion process has been studied in several settings, and many questions remain open.

Scope

This site and the accompanying repository provide a place to develop and share our understanding of these results. We aim to bring together expositions and formalisations, and to explore further directions as a community.
