Cluster formation from dust on the complete graph

Overview · Digested · Raw

From a homogeneous start to clusters

Let \(N\) particles occupy \(L\) sites of a complete graph. Take \(N/L\to\rho\in(0,\infty)\) and \(Ld_L\to\theta\in(0,\infty)\). Assume the largest initial occupation is \(o(N)\). This includes the homogeneous start, where the site occupations differ by at most one.

The ranked occupation fractions converge coordinatewise to the zero sequence at time zero. This sequence represents dust, or mass in sites with vanishing occupation fractions. The limiting Poisson–Dirichlet diffusion starts at this sequence. Almost surely, its positive coordinates carry total mass one at every \(t>0\). Chleboun, Gabriel and Grosskinsky (2024), Proposition 1.3 and Corollary 1.4

Research question

How do the first clusters form in the particle system? For times \(t_L\downarrow0\), can we identify the scale and joint law of the largest occupations? Can an AI-assisted calculation relate this regime to the small-time law of the Poisson–Dirichlet diffusion?
