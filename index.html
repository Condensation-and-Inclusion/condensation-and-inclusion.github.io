Condensation and Inclusion

Home · Topics · Literature

We study condensation in the inclusion process. Particles jump at a rate that increases with the number of particles at the destination. This attraction can concentrate mass on a small fraction of sites. Grosskinsky, Redig and Vafayi (2011)

Scope

We study sticky mass flow on the circle, the first clusters on the complete graph, and coarsening on a ring with jumps in one direction. The Topics pages give the problem statements, proofs, and research notes.
